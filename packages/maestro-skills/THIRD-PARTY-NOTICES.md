## Addy Osmani

These skills restate the matching addyosmani/agent-skills skills in Maestro's
words, with the matching checklists and references as sidecars. Paths are
relative to `packages/maestro-skills/skills/`.

- `maestro-frontend-ui/SKILL.md` and `maestro-frontend-ui/accessibility.md`
  (`frontend-ui-engineering`, `accessibility-checklist`).
- `maestro-idea-refine/SKILL.md`, `maestro-idea-refine/frameworks.md` and
  `maestro-idea-refine/refinement-criteria.md` (`idea-refine`).
- `maestro-observability/SKILL.md` and `maestro-observability/checklist.md`
  (`observability-and-instrumentation`, `observability-checklist`).
- `maestro-performance/SKILL.md` and
  `maestro-performance/optimization-patterns.md` (`performance-optimization`,
  `performance-checklist`).
- `maestro-quality-gates/SKILL.md` (`constraint-driven-development`).
- `maestro-security/SKILL.md` and `maestro-security/hardening-patterns.md`
  (`security-and-hardening`).
- `maestro-ship/SKILL.md` (`shipping-and-launch`).
